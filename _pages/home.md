Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Political Science at Michigan State University.
My research examines political accountability, representation, and political geography in Africa.

I study how the geography of politics shapes the way citizens experience and make sense of the political world. I am particularly interested in how local political environments shape citizens' understandings of political representation and accountability. I also explore how everyday interactions with the state influence the ways citizens understand who holds political power. 

I am also a research assistant for the Analysis Unit at Afrobarometer. My analyses can be found 
<a href="https://www.afrobarometer.org/person/sophie-sunderland/" target="_blank">here</a>.
I previously earned both my B.A. and M.A. in Political Science from the University of Florida, where I also completed minors in Latin American and African Studies.
